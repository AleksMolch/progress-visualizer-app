Privacy Policy — Progress Visualizer

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Progress Visualizer ("we", "our", "the app") is a privacy-first photo tracking application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about data handling.

Русская версия: PRIVACY.md.

1. Data collection

What the app stores (on your device only)

  • Photos you take or import;
  • Project names and photo notes you create;
  • App settings and preferences;
  • Biometric protection flag (if enabled).

What the app does NOT collect

  • Your photos are never uploaded to any server;
  • No personal information (name, email, phone);
  • No analytics or tracking;
  • No advertising identifiers;
  • No location data.

How data is stored

All data stays locally on your device: photos — in the app's private sandbox directory; metadata and settings — in encrypted local storage (MMKV with an encryption key kept in the system Keychain / SecureStore).

2. Permissions

  • Camera — required to take progress photos.
  • Photo library (optional) — used only when you choose to import a photo from your device or export a photo to the gallery.
  • Biometrics (optional) — Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint, only if you enable the privacy lock. Biometric data never leaves your device.
  • Notifications (optional) — local reminders only, scheduled on the device; no push server.

3. Data security

  • All data is stored locally in the app sandbox and encrypted storage;
  • Biometric authentication uses the system Secure Enclave / Keystore;
  • There is no cloud synchronization and no network layer for photos or metadata.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • You control all data;
  • You can delete projects and photos at any time in the app;
  • Uninstalling the app removes all app data from the device.

5. Export to gallery

If you explicitly tap "Export", the selected photo is copied to the device gallery. After that it is outside the app's protected sandbox and may be accessible to other apps. The app warns you before exporting.

6. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. It is intended for general audiences.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be published with a new "Last updated" date. The current version is kept in the project repository.

8. Contact

For privacy questions: al.molch@gmail.com

9. GDPR & CCPA compliance

Data subject rights:

  • Right to access — view all your data in the app;
  • Right to delete — delete projects/photos in-app or uninstall the app;
  • Right to portability — export photos to the gallery;
  • No sale of personal data (we collect none).

Legal basis:

  • Consent — by using the app you consent to local data storage;
  • Legitimate interest — providing the photo tracking functionality.
